Selected Work

Decisions behind shipped platforms.

Three enterprise stories, each told through the call I made, what it cost, and what changed.

01 · AI search

Reimagining search for luxury commerce

Alexander Wang · Global DTC & Omnichannel

-42% zero-result searches
XGen AISFCCMerchandisingAnalytics
Problem
Luxury customers hit weak relevance, merchandising gaps and dead-end searches.
Decision
Treat zero-result handling, synonyms and redirects as launch requirements, not post-launch tuning, and keep ranking controls in merchandising's hands.
Tradeoff
Algorithmic relevance versus brand control over how a luxury catalog surfaces. I scoped requirements so the AI did the heavy lifting while merchandisers could still steer results.
Evidence
42% fewer zero-result searches, plus an operating model for continuously tuning search performance after launch.

02 · Platform & workflows

Client, coach and admin platforms

Ezra Coaching · B2B SaaS · First product manager

-35% time to first coaching session
OnboardingCoach matchingInternal toolsDesign system
Problem
Three connected products (enterprise clients, coaches, participants) created friction at every handoff.
Decision
Anchor the roadmap on time to first session, and prioritize onboarding, coach matching and scheduling changes against that one measure.
Tradeoff
Speed on individual products versus investing in a shared design system across all three. I chose shared patterns so fixes landed everywhere at once.
Evidence
35% faster time to first coaching session, and a shared design system across the product suite.

03 · Shared capabilities at scale

Enterprise promotions and global release

Estée Lauder Companies Online

260+sites
30+brands
2,100+tickets / year
Promotions engineRelease governanceUATInternal tooling
Problem
Dozens of brand teams needed complex, market-specific promotions on a shared platform without breaking each other's releases.
Decision
Rebuild promotions as a shared internal platform for global brand teams, backed by one release governance process for every site.
Tradeoff
Brand-level flexibility versus shared governance. Standard capabilities and release gates kept 30+ brands moving without one-off builds.
Evidence
Release governance, QA coordination and UAT readiness across 260+ sites and 2,100+ tickets a year.
