Practice Coach

A conversational AI coach for the moments at work you want to handle better.

I was the first product manager at Ezra, a coaching marketplace. Practice Coach is that domain rebuilt AI-first: the coach is the conversation, not a scheduling layer around a human session.

  1. Pick a modeWork coaching, or opt into Wellness
  2. Talk it throughThe coach asks before it advises, using coaching frameworks from published research
  3. Leave with one stepEvery session ends with something to practice
What I'm evaluating
  • Does it ask before it tells?
  • Does it hold context across a session?
  • Does it know when to point someone to a human?

Product callWork coaching stands on its own. Wellness is opt-in, for people who want support beyond the job.
